## Anti-Patterns Verdict
LLM: Game now has clear identity — theatrical indie game, not AI-generated dark app. Cinzel on gold + die shapes + animated entrance = intentional authorship. Die shapes bypass jargon with real-world tabletop language.

## What's Working
1. Entrance animation earns its place — title rising gives homescreen gravity.
2. Die shapes replace jargon without losing information — triangle+orange = learnable symbol.
3. Danger pulse changes emotional register of low-HP play — pre-cognitive stakes communication.
4. Camera fades make game feel authored — every transition signals "someone decided this."

## Remaining Priority Issues

**[P2] Game over screen communicates nothing about the run**
Shows "GAME OVER" + tap-to-retry. No battles won, no cause of death, no final HP. For a roguelike the run summary IS part of the game — it's how players learn.
Fix: Add 3 lines — battles won (battleIndex), last enemy (enemyDef.name), cause of death. Already available.
Command: /impeccable harden or /impeccable onboard

**[P2] DiceDuel whose-turn state is ambient, not explicit**
Turn banner appears briefly then disappears. After handoff, player holding device can't tell whose turn it is at a glance.
Fix: Persistent colored header band showing current player throughout their turn. Player 1 = gold, Player 2 = cyan.
Command: /impeccable clarify
